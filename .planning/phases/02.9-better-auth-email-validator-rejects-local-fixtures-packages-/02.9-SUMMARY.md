# Phase 02.9 Summary — RFC-Compliant Fixture Email (Cascade Tail #9)

| **`rotation-test@local`**     | **FAIL** | **No TLD (single-label host)** |
| `rotation-test@example.com`   | OK     | RFC 2606 reserved TLD valid  |
