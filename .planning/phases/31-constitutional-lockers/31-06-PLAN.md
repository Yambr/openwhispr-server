|---|---|
| Negative lookbehind on `\.exec(` misses obscure forms like `(regex).exec(value)` | Acceptable; the parens still place a `.` before `exec`. Edge case `[regex][0].exec(value)` also has `.` before exec. |
| Pass-2 5-line peek misses multi-line template literals | Increase peek window to 10 lines; document in locker header. |
| `exec(` matches keyword `exec` in a comment or docstring | Acceptable false-positive rate; allowlist absorbs. Optionally strip line-comments first (defer to refactor). |
| Two-pass regex is stateful and hurts coverage on edge paths | Aim for explicit `findViolations` returning `Violation[]` with branch coverage covering the negative-lookbehind, multi-line peek, and same-statement boundary. |
